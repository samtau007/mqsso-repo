Asked,
answered.

Can I read the Qur’an on Muslim Quotient?No. It holds no content. You read and learn on the platforms you choose, and they add to your picture.
Is my Muslim Quotient a score?It is a range on a 0 to 10 ruler. You see your own range. No one is ranked.
What does it cost?Muslim Quotient is free. Some connected platforms, such as Mohasaba, may charge for their own service.
Can I delete everything?Yes. Disconnect any platform, export your picture, or delete your account and everything in it.
